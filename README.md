I am **Changhai Zhou (周昌海)** (`zhouchanghai` / `changhaizhou` / `changhaizhou98`), a PhD student (combined Master's–PhD program, enrolled in 2023) at **Fudan University**. I have hands-on experience across the **full stack of trillion-parameter (T-scale) foundation models**, spanning **pre-training, mid-training, and post-training**. Earlier, my work focused on **model architecture**, while more recently I have focused on **training efficiency** and **data-driven** foundation model scaling.

I have interned with **Baidu's ERNIE team**, **Meituan's LongCat team**, and **ByteDance's foundation model team**, participating in the end-to-end training and iteration of trillion-parameter foundation models across pre-training, mid-training, and post-training. At Fudan University, my academic research covers parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), model compression, adaptive inference, and multi-tenant LLM serving.
